## inheritance.ts

1. `sexLinkedReport(v)`: keys model='xr'|'xd'|'y', mother='AA'|'Aa'|'aa', father='A'|'a'. In xr a is disease allele; xd A is disease allele. Female offspring inherit father's X and random maternal allele; sons inherit maternal X. Equal sex probability. Output genotype probabilities with sex labels, affected probability among ALL children and conditional among daughters and among sons. Carrier distinction only xr heterozygous female. Y model father A=unaffected/a=affected (mother ignored). Y trait only father->all sons, never daughters. Validate allowed strings.

2. `bloodReport(v)`: mother/father in 'AA','AO','BB','BO','AB','OO'. Alleles A/B codominant, O recessive, outputs genotype and phenotype distributions, display Iᴬ / Iᴮ / i in explanation. Test AO×BO produces A/B/AB/O each .25, AB×OO yields A/B each .5, invalid rejects. Explain phenotype A may be AA or AO, so users select genotypes; not forensic parentage determination.

3. `pedigreeReport(v)`: keys mother/father='healthy'|'affected', childSex='female'|'male', child='healthy'|'affected'. Enumerate all parental genotypes compatible with their phenotype for five models AR, AD, XR, XD, Y; keep a model if at least one parental pairing has nonzero probability of stated child phenotype and sex. Report candidate models in table, optional feasible parental genotypes. Do NOT assign probabilities across candidate models or claim uniqueness beyond these restrictive assumptions. Notes: one nuclear family, complete penetrance, no mutation, no phenocopies; candidates not diagnosis, not complete multigeneration pedigree. Test unaffected parents affected daughter => only AR, unaffected parents affected son => AR/XR; affected father normal mother normal son should exclude Y; all-normal retains five.
